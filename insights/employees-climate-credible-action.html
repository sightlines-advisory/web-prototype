EXPLAINER · STAKEHOLDER PERSPECTIVES

Employees want credible action and a future they can work towards

What workforce expectations and capabilities mean for delivering business change.

Susan McHattie | Sightlines AdvisoryNovember 20262 min read (estimated)First disclosure

Explore this subject further: What support is available for transition?.

Your people help determine your success. They know how operations work, where resources are wasted and when change can generate value. They also hold expectations about the organisation they work for and their own future within it.

Deloitte’s 2025 Australian Gen Z and Millennial Survey found:

Environmental expectations sit alongside immediate employment needs.

Deloitte’s 2026 Australian Gen Z and Millennial Survey Australian findings identified cost of living as the leading concern for both cohorts. That context matters when leaders ask people to support changes to equipment, processes, roles and skills.

Worker representatives bring job security directly into the discussion.

“Australians want action on climate change, and they also want good secure jobs in their communities.”

Michele O’Neil, ACTU President, August 2026.

For leaders, the task is to connect the company’s direction with your people. Explain why a change matters, involve those who will implement it and identify the training, resources and adjustments required.

Employees surface opportunities that financial or climate analysis might miss. The maintenance team know the equipment that might be replaced to increase efficiency. Customer-facing staff recognise changing purchasing requirements and patterns before they appear in revenue forecasts. Create opportunities for your people to understand the climate influence on company value, and shape your response.

Three questions for leaders

  • What do our people understand about the business’s direction and their role in it?
  • What evidence do we have about environmental expectations in recruitment, retention and everyday operations?
  • Which planned responses depend on new skills, changed work and employee cooperation?

Sightlines Advisory brings those perspectives into the Climate Value Framework©. We help leaders connect workforce knowledge and capability with the commercial case, implementation plan and measures of progress.
