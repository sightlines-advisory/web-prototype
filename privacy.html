Privacy

How we handle website information and contact enquiries.

What we collect

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, including your name, email address and enquiry. We use it to respond, provide requested services and manage our business relationship.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website, such as pages viewed, device and browser information, and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies. We use this information to improve the website and do not use it to identify you personally.

How we use and protect information

We use information only for legitimate business purposes, protect it with proportionate technical and organisational controls, and do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers, including Google, that process information on our behalf.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google’s opt-out tools for Google Analytics. To ask about personal information we hold, contact us at hello@sightlines-advisory.com.au.
